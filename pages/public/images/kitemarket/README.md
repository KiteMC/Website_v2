# KiteMarket website images

The product icon is copied from the editable `KiteMarket-Source/branding/` artwork.

To fill the six screenshot slots, add real PNG gameplay captures with these names:

1. `screenshot-home.png`
2. `screenshot-market.png`
3. `screenshot-rule-editor.png`
4. `screenshot-supply-preview.png`
5. `screenshot-auction-confirm.png`
6. `screenshot-wallet.png`

Chinese and English pages share the same image paths and provide localized captions.
No image is needed to publish the documentation. The reusable
`ScreenshotPlaceholder` component shows an explained empty frame until its PNG
loads successfully; missing or broken images never appear as a broken-image icon.
Do not include license keys, credentials, player private information or fake
gameplay screenshots.
